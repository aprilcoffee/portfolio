Imaginary Landscape (Performance)

2023

The performance version of the project Imaginary Landscape (2022–2024). AI-generated images of idealised beaches, pieced together from travel photos on social media, run across a wide projection while the sound is played live: an “ideal other side” that is never reached.

Watch on Vimeo ↗

valentinstag

2024

soft rotation, Valentine’s Day edition, with 674FM, 14 February 2024. A set about talking with a chatbot about what love is, through granular synthesis. soft rotation is a concert series organised by students of the Academy of Media Arts Cologne (KHM).

Browse

2019

Nuit Blanche – Lacking Sound Festival #Soirée, Winsing Art Place, Taipei, 14 September 2019. Web pages are taken apart into source code and outlines, and played as image and sound.

Watch on Vimeo ↗

Lift-Off Vol. 3

2019

The third of four versions of Lift-Off, built on the cloud left by the SpaceX Falcon 9 launch of SAOCOM-1A. Sound in Max 8, four-channel video in Processing 3.4. Opening of the TNUA New Media Arts graduation show “Reentry”, Songshan Cultural and Creative Park, Taipei, 14 June 2019.

Watch on Vimeo ↗

Lift-off

2018

An earlier version of Lift-Off. On 7 October 2018 the SpaceX Falcon 9 launch of SAOCOM-1A left a glowing cloud over the US west coast; the set turns that cloud into sound and image.

Watch on Vimeo ↗

Anesthesia Calculations

Opening performance of “A/P”, the annual show of the New Media Arts department at TNUA, Taipei, 2016. An early audio-visual set from student years.

Untitled

2016

Noise performance at TNUA, Taipei, 19 April 2016. The only sound sources are electromagnetic waves and a mixer.
