# Teaching, education and awards

Academic positions, studies, awards and fellowships, as listed in the CV.

## Teaching

At Bauhaus University Weimar Liu mainly teaches creative coding, critical AI and interactive video installation (the artist, 2026); his teaching follows a line of thinking about cybernetics.

**This semester (winter 2026/27):** the seminar *Seeing Like a Machine: Telling Stories With and Against How AI
Judges Images*, part one of a two-semester seminar funded by Stifterverband / eTeach-Netzwerk Thüringen. Following
Harun Farocki's idea of the operational image, students learn basic Python to cut, process and sort archival footage
from the internet, turn frames into text, sort an archive by meaning instead of keywords, and treat the machine's
mistakes and biases as material rather than errors; for example, asking it for the frames that best match "ice cream"
in footage that has nothing to do with ice cream, and building a story from what it finds. Weekly readings and
artworks, a humorous approach with a critical position. The second semester moves to projects and a shared public
exhibition. Readings: [Seminar reading list](topics/seminar-reading-list.md).
