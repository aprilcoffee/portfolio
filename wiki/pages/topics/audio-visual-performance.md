# Audio-visual performance

Live audio-visual sets (for example *Lift-Off*: sound in Max 8, four-channel video in Processing 3.4), recorded on
Vimeo and listed in the "Performance archive" at the bottom of the website's Works page (the separate Performance page was merged into Works in October 2026; Liu no longer focuses on live performance). Examples: *Imaginary Landscape* (from the project
of the same name), *valentinstag* (soft rotation, Valentine's Day edition, with 674FM, 14 February 2024: a set about talking with a chatbot about what love is, through granular synthesis; event page: https://softrotation.substack.com/p/soft-rotation-3), *Browse* (Nuit Blanche – Lacking
Sound Festival, Taipei, 2019), *Lift-Off* (four versions, based on the SpaceX Falcon 9 launch cloud of
7 October 2018; see the blog post), and early noise performances at TNUA (2016).

Festivals in the CV include moers festival (2025; a noise set, not recorded), Blaues Rauschen (2023) and Lacking Sound Festival
(2017, 2019). → [Exhibitions and performances](../exhibitions-and-performances.md)
