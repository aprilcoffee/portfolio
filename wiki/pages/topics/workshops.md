# Workshops

Liu's workshops are about using AI locally, working with embeddings by hand, and seeing the biases inside AI models. Requests go to ting-chun.liu@uni-weimar.de. Three workshops are described on the website's Workshops page (https://liutingchun.com/workshops/).

## Condition for Emotions (2026)

Participants install small AI models on their own laptops and ask them questions such as "How icecream is the word red compared to blue?" Embeddings turn words, sentences and images into positions in a space, and distances in that space become the machine's answers. A small web tool built for the workshop measures the distance between sentences, places words on a line between two opposite words, compares token counts across languages, follows a model as it continues a sentence, swaps one name in a sentence to show bias, and asks a vision model about an image. Held on 13 June 2026 in Münster for the DFG network "Das Wissen der digitalen Literatur", in the context of Alphabete der Automatisierung. Announced: 13 November 2026 at KHM, 17 December 2026 at TH Köln (Code & Context).

## Paranoia De-Prompting (2026, with Leon-Etienne Kühr)

A hands-on introduction to CLIP, the model that scores how well an image and a text match. Participants score images against short texts (a painting of a pipe, a cat in three languages, a generated face against emojis), then run a loop that pulls random generated faces and keeps the highest and lowest CLIP scores for a word such as "happy", "nurse" or "programmer". Held at the mtrail Ausbildungscamp in Lindau and at KISD (Köln International School of Design). The same title was also given as a talk at the University of Bonn on 16 July 2026, invited by the Junge GI Hochschulgruppe Bonn (see [Talks and writing](../talks-and-writing.md)).

## The Given Image (2025)

A telephone game (Stille Post) for images: a group describes the image it is given, an image generator turns the prompt back into a picture, and the new picture goes to the next group, who see neither the prompt nor the original. CLIP shows a similarity score as a small cue. Held at Café Nimmersatt in Wolfhagen, at the invitation of Dawid Liftinger, who was artist in residence at the Walter-Lübcke-Schule in Wolfhagen (programme "Das fliegende Künstlerzimmer", seasons 2023/24 and 2024/25). The game is also used in Liu's teaching at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar. A workshop run on 17 April 2026 (8 groups, 5 rounds) produced chains such as a meatball stew that drifts into an orange soup with potatoes and a fork. The prototype is open source: https://github.com/aprilcoffee/ai-stille-post/tree/main/stille_post
