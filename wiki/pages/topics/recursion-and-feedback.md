# Recursion and feedback

A recurring method: let a system confront its own output and watch where it drifts. The work texts
describe recursion as "a spiral that surpasses linear causation", and feedback as the microphone held
to a speaker, where the result shows the room as much as the machine. Alvin Lucier's *I Am Sitting in
a Room* (1969) is a named reference.

## Works

- [Steering Through the Inner Residue](../works/steering-through-the-inner-residue.md): image generation without prompts, guided only by previous images.
- [Latent Heat Generation](../works/latent-heat-generation.md): GPUs reading their own heat and turning it into images.
- [Self-cannibalizing AI](../works/self-cannibalizing-ai.md): models learning from each other's output.

## In the thesis and in others' writing

- In [Heat as (Generative) Image Making](../texts/heat-as-generative-image-making.md), recursion is the method of the "purple coincidence" (an image model looped on its own output without a prompt drifts into purple noise) and the frame of the whole text is cybernetic: the loss function is "a self-correcting feedback loop that is cybernetic in principle".
- Violaine Boutet de Monvel's essay on model collapse, which compares analog video feedback with recursive AI training (arXiv:2607.09705), places Liu and Kühr among artists exploring this ground and cites their TEDA'24 poster: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09705
- Talks: the 37C3 recording of [Self-cannibalizing AI](../works/self-cannibalizing-ai.md), and the Screen Walk of 26 March 2025 (https://thephotographersgallery.org.uk/whats-on/watch-screen-walk-leon-etienne-kuhr-and-ting-chun-liu).
