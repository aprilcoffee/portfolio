# Steering Through the Inner Residue

A video installation with Leon-Etienne Kühr in which an image model is steered by nothing but its own previous output. The text prompt is dropped entirely, so each generated image becomes the next input and the process drifts like a feedback loop, replacing human expectations with the machine's own.

## Key ideas

- AI as a black box whose inner workings show only through what it produces.
- Recursion as a spiral rather than repetition: each loop deviates slightly and ends at unknown coordinates.
- Reference to Alvin Lucier's *I Am Sitting in a Room* (1969), where re-recording lets the room's resonance replace speech.
