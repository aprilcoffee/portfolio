# Bring Us Somewhere (帶我去一個地方)

A video from 2020, commissioned for the exhibition *Urban Interpreter Online* (城市轉藝器) at 241 Art Gallery in
Hsinchu, Taiwan. According to the artist, the work is about what a city is. The exhibition (20 November 2020 – 7 March 2021) was organised by the Hsinchu City Government and curated by 蔡奇宏; it invited local teams and young digital artists to act as the city's "translation parameters". Other participants included Dimension Plus and 林瑜亮 Lin Yu-Liang (of [LLL](../people/lll.md)).
