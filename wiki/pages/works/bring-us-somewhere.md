# Bring Us Somewhere (帶我去一個地方)

A video from 2020, commissioned for the exhibition *Urban Interpreter Online* (城市轉藝器) at 241 Art Gallery in
Hsinchu, Taiwan. According to the artist, the work is about what a city is.

## Facts

- **Year:** 2020
- **Type:** Video, commission
- **Exhibition:** Urban Interpreter Online (城市轉藝器), 241 Art Gallery, Hsinchu (listed in the CV under Exhibitions, 2020)
