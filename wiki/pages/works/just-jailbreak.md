# Just Jailbreak

An interactive game around a large language model. Players have to talk JJ, a stubborn character who imitates technology but refuses to work, into cooperating, an introduction to the culture of LLM 'jailbreaking'. It borrows the Taiwanese Kuai Kuai (乖乖) custom of placing the snack on machines to make them 'obedient'.

## Facts

- **Year:** 2024
- **Type:** Interactive game about jailbreaking a large language model
- **Type (de):** Interaktives Spiel über das Jailbreaken eines großen Sprachmodells
- **Type (zh):** 關於大型語言模型越獄的互動遊戲
- **With:** Fabian Mosele (the artist confirms the collaboration; Synthiola is Fabian Mosele's project)

## Key ideas

- Jailbreaking as playful negotiation: being firm, mischievous, bribing, or affectionate.
- A local Taiwanese tech superstition reframed for AI chatbots.
