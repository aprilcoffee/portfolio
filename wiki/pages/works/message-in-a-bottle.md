# Message in a Bottle (瓶中信)

A series of Facebook posts (2017) whose only content is the exact time of posting, e.g. "Mon 01:56:23". Each post is a message in a bottle labelled with its own birthday, floating on the ocean of social media until someone fills it with a story.

## Key ideas

- What is posting for, if content hardly matters?
- The precise timestamp replaces the system's vague '32 mins ago'.
