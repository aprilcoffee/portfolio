# Poetic Materialization – Regarding Midjourney (essay, 2022)

An essay by Ting Chun Liu in *Disruptive News 22* (16 October 2022), a newsletter of [ ] ground zero at the Academy of Media Arts Cologne, in an issue about "wordless words", artificial language systems and their cultural effects. It follows text-to-image generators from GPT-2 and DALL·E to Midjourney and asks what happens to an artist's work when it becomes words typed into a box.

## Key ideas

- **Transformers and zero-shot prompting.** The transformer framework changed how people deal with AI: an output can be asked for without examples. "Prompt engineering" becomes a new syntax between natural language and commands.
- **Labour turned into heat.** "The artist's labor is reduced to the input of words, the emission of heat, and finally, production." Generating images uses large computing resources, so human effort ends as thermal energy. This is the earliest text on the wiki that joins prompts and heat, the thread of [Heat as (Generative) Image Making](heat-as-generative-image-making.md).
- **Levelling.** Hundreds of images a second saturate the visual field and flatten value.
- **Imagination stays.** Midjourney's stereotyped outputs for some prompts show limits set by prejudiced training data.
- **Not replacement but network.** Using Bruno Latour's actor-network idea and 1960s conceptual art, where viewers complete the work, Liu reads such systems as collaborators in an expanded network of creative labour.
- **Models and systems named:** GPT-2, GPT-3, CLIP, DALL·E and DALL·E 2, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, Disco Diffusion, Google's Imagen, GANs.
