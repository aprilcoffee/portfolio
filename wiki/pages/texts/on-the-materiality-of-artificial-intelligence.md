# On the Materiality of Artificial Intelligence (text and talk)

Liu's short text on the physical side of AI, printed in volume 3 of *un/learn ai – integrating AI in aesthetic practices* (KITeGG research project, 2025, pp. 218–223) and read online on the project's website. The same title was Liu's lecture at the Correlations forum in Offenbach in December 2024. The wiki has not been able to read the article text itself, so this page records where it is and what the same ground looks like elsewhere in Liu's work.

## Facts

- **Text:** *On the Materiality of Artificial Intelligence*, in: *un/learn ai – integrating AI in aesthetic practices*, vol. 3, pp. 218–223, Forschungsprojekt KITeGG, 2025. Online article: https://unlearn.gestaltung.ai/de/article/gpm91yc8?chunks=gpm91yc8
- **Volume:** edited by Matthias Grund, Johanna Teresa Wallenborn, Lasse Scherffig and Florian Jenett; open access, CC BY 4.0; DOI 10.25358/openscience-13020; repository page https://openscience.ub.uni-mainz.de/items/7d8cb85f-0e1a-409e-b9a1-a9dac96e48a0
- **Talk:** *On the Materiality of Artificial Intelligence*, Correlations – Forum für KI in Kunst und Design, HfG Offenbach, 11–13 December 2024 (free admission). https://ground-zero.khm.de/correlations/
- **Project behind the book:** KITeGG ("KI greifbar machen und begreifen: Technologie und Gesellschaft verbinden durch Gestaltung"), five design schools, funded by the BMBF and four German states. See [KITeGG and un/learn ai](../institutions/kitegg-unlearn-ai.md).

## Where the same ideas are written out

The thesis chapter "Materiality of Artificial Intelligence" covers GPUs, quartz from Spruce Pine, wafers, EUV lithography, TSMC and the Taiwan dilemma: see [Heat as (Generative) Image Making](heat-as-generative-image-making.md). The talk and the text sit in the same line as the 38C3 talk *arafed futures*, which maps embedding spaces against the semiconductor supply chain ([Chaos Communication Congress](../institutions/chaos-communication-congress.md)).
