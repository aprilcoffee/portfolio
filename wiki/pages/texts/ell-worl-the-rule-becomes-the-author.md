# »_ell_ Worl_!« – the rule becomes the author (2021)

A co-authored paper by Christian Heck, Ting Chun Liu, Mattis Kuhn, Tiago Ive Rubini and Julia Nakotte (all Academy of
Media Arts Cologne; Rubini also cAt, GIIP – UNESP), presented at the 10th Encontro Internacional de Grupos de Pesquisa
("convergências entre arte, ciência, tecnologias & realidades mistas", UNESP, São Paulo, 2021) and listed on the KHM
platform [ ] ground zero – poetics of technics and cognition.

- **Subject:** writing code as a form of literature. The paper is built around *Beatnik*, a stack-based esoteric
  programming language (esolang) created by Cliff L. Biffle in 2001, in which each word has a value. The authors wrote
  their own Beatnik, "designed mainly by Ting Chun Liu and Christian Heck", and the poem »_ell_ Worl_!« with its
  interpreter.
- **Argument:** authorship as a sociotechnical network (Bruno Latour's Actor-Network Theory; Eric Raymond's
  "constructive laziness"): algorithms are not authors in the traditional sense, but the fixed rules for generating the
  text, the algorithms as actors and the reuse of other texts decide the "new" text. The paper links writing through
  algorithms to modern literature, the Beat generation and Gertrude Stein, and to life in the pandemic, when everyday
  life was increasingly formalised as data in vector spaces.
- **Keywords (paper):** art and technology, programming, open source, esolang, literature.
