# Frequently asked questions

Short answers to questions visitors are likely to ask. Each answer links to the page it comes from.

**Who is Ting-Chun Liu?**
An artist and artistic researcher from Taipei, Taiwan, working in Germany on critical AI, audiovisual
media and network practice. → [Overview](overview.md)

**What is the work about?**
Mostly about how AI systems see, sort and generate, and what that hides: biases in image models,
feedback loops, the heat and chips behind computation, and how institutions assign meaning.
→ [Critical AI](topics/critical-ai-and-image-models.md), [Recursion](topics/recursion-and-feedback.md),
[Material infrastructure](topics/material-infrastructure-of-ai.md)

**Which works are the most recent?**
Steering Through the Inner Residue (2026), Interpolation of the Invisible Color (2025),
Latent Heat Generation (2025). → [Overview](overview.md#recent-works)

**Who does Liu work with?**
Most AI-related works and talks since 2023 are made with Leon-Etienne Kühr. Early light installations
were made as the collective LLL. → [Leon-Etienne Kühr](people/leon-etienne-kuehr.md), [LLL](people/lll.md)

**Where did Liu study and teach?**
B.F.A. at Taipei National University of the Arts (2014–2018), Postgraduate Diploma at the Academy of
Media Arts Cologne (2020–2025, with distinction); teaching at Bauhaus University Weimar since 2024/2025
and taught in Vienna in 2026 through Erasmus+ Staff Mobility for Teaching (University of Applied Arts Vienna). → [Teaching, education and awards](teaching-education-awards.md)

**Has Liu won awards?**
Weizenbaum-Studienpreis 2025 (FIfF e.V.) for the thesis "Heat as Generative Image Making"; a 2026
Stifterverband fellowship for digital university teaching in Thuringia. → [FIfF](institutions/fiff.md)

**Where can I watch the talks?**
The three Chaos Communication Congress talks are on media.ccc.de: 37C3 https://media.ccc.de/v/37c3-12125-self-cannibalizing_ai, 38C3 https://media.ccc.de/v/38c3-arafed-futures-an-artist-dialogue-on-chip-storage-and-ai-accelerationism, 39C3 https://media.ccc.de/v/39c3-51-ways-to-spell-the-image-giraffe-the-hidden-politics-of-token-languages-in-generative-ai. The Screen Walk of 26 March 2025 is at https://thephotographersgallery.org.uk/whats-on/watch-screen-walk-leon-etienne-kuhr-and-ting-chun-liu
→ [Talks and writing](talks-and-writing.md)

**Where can I read Liu's own texts?**
The diploma thesis is a website: https://aprilcoffee.github.io/heat_as_image/index.html (summary: [thesis](texts/heat-as-generative-image-making.md)). *Stereotype Encoding* (with Leon-Etienne Kühr) is on Academia.edu ([summary](texts/stereotype-encoding.md)); *On the Materiality of Artificial Intelligence* is in the open-access book *un/learn ai*, vol. 3 ([page](texts/on-the-materiality-of-artificial-intelligence.md)). More in [External links](external-links.md).

**Is Liu's work cited or shown by others?**
Yes: Violaine Boutet de Monvel's essay on model collapse (arXiv:2607.09705) cites Liu and Kühr's feedback experiments; *Latent Heat Generation* was part of *Cybernetic Subjects* at the Ars Electronica Festival 2025; Liu and Kühr were among 17 artists in *AI-Worlding* (Museum Angewandte Kunst, Frankfurt, 2026). → [External links](external-links.md)

**Where are the videos of the performances?**
In the "Performance archive" at the bottom of the website's Works page; each record opens on Vimeo. Liu no longer focuses on live performance, so these are recordings from 2016–2024. → https://liutingchun.com/works/#performances

**Where are the talk recordings?**
On the website's "Talks & Workshops" page, below the workshops (39C3, 38C3, 37C3, Screen Walk). Texts by others about the work are listed in the CV on the About page. → https://liutingchun.com/workshops/#talks · https://liutingchun.com/about/#texts

**Is the blog available in English?**
No. The blog is an archive of posts from 2017–2022 in Mandarin, including Raspberry Pi and Processing
tutorials. → [Blog archive](blog-archive.md)

**Does Liu give workshops? How can I invite Liu for a talk, workshop, exhibition or collaboration?**
Yes: Liu hosts workshops about local AI usage, embedding practice and understanding biases in AI models, and gives lectures and talks (see [Talks and writing](talks-and-writing.md) and [Workshops](topics/workshops.md)). The workshops are flexible: they work for people who have never written code, artists, creators, companies or literary scholars, and what participants take away is mainly AI literacy. New topics can be discussed, for example anything around tokens. Requests for workshops, talks, collaborations and commissions go by email to ting-chun.liu@uni-weimar.de; Liu is open to them. Prices and availability are not in the wiki.

**What is Liu's practice about, in his own words?**
He approaches AI art along media art's question of medium specificity and asks how to go beyond simply prompting: for example by making images with no prompt at all, feeding a model's output back into itself the way Alvin Lucier re-recorded his voice in a room. From there the questions extend to geopolitics, data politics and the operational image. → [Medium specificity](topics/medium-specificity-and-practice.md)

**Where does Liu live and work? Which languages?**
He works in Cologne and Weimar, mainly in English and also in Mandarin. He came to Germany in 2019. → [Overview](overview.md)

**Will AI replace artists?**
Liu's answer is no: artists use AI to interact with the technical objects around them. → [Medium specificity](topics/medium-specificity-and-practice.md)

**What does the wiki not know?**
Anything that is not on the website or in the sources listed on each page: private life (which Liu keeps private), prices,
availability, unpublished projects. The chat should say so instead of guessing.

## Lighter questions (the artist's answers, October 2026)

**What ice cream does Ting-Chun like most?**
Sea salt. The question is fitting: "How ice cream is the word red, compared to blue?" is the opening question of his
workshop [Conditions for Emotions](topics/workshops.md), where small language models answer such absurd questions with
full confidence.

**Favourite city?** Cologne.

**What is he reading?** Vilém Flusser and Harun Farocki (October 2026). For reading tips on a theme, see the
[seminar reading list](topics/seminar-reading-list.md).

**What does he teach this semester?** The seminar *Seeing Like a Machine: Telling Stories With and Against How AI
Judges Images* at Bauhaus University Weimar (winter 2026/27). → [Teaching](teaching-education-awards.md)

**What does he do outside work?** He plays Hearthstone.

**A quote he likes?** Sol LeWitt: "Obviously a drawing of a person is not a real person, but a drawing of a line is a
real line." (He remembers it as being about a painted flower and a drawn line; the wording above is the one attributed
to LeWitt.)

**How did he get into AI?** He started by playing with natural language processing; in 2022 he tried the earliest
DALL·E and found the idea of joining text and images interesting.

**How does a work start?** From the aesthetics of a technical framework. Methods besides feedback: research context,
historical stories, experiments and tests, hacking, overtuning.

**Which work is hardest to explain?** 51 Ways to Spell the Image Giraffe, because so many technical questions have to
be described; in his words, all the works need a lot of prior knowledge to be understood.
→ [51 Ways to Spell the Image Giraffe](works/51-ways-to-spell-the-image-giraffe.md)

**How do Ting-Chun and Leon-Etienne Kühr work together?** They discuss with each other and write code together.
→ [Leon-Etienne Kühr](people/leon-etienne-kuehr.md)

**Which tools?** All of them: p5.js, Processing, Python, TouchDesigner and others. He uses generative AI himself when
writing code.

**Will AI replace artists?** No. Artists adapt new technologies and aesthetics; whatever happens, they have to stay
experimental and must not be bound by the technology.

**Semiconductors and geopolitics?** For him it is a contradictory concept, not simply about the environment or labour;
geopolitics is in it too. Artists should not handle a single issue but discuss this many-sided contradiction, which is
something only art can do. → [Material infrastructure of AI](topics/material-infrastructure-of-ai.md)

**Local or cloud models?** Both. Local small models mainly in teaching, because students have no budget and should still
be able to use AI.

**Workshops: format?** On participants' computers or in the cloud; mainly in English and Chinese; the length can be
discussed. → [Workshops](topics/workshops.md)

**Loans, commissions, collections, citing works, using images, interviews, podcasts, students' questions?** All
welcome by email (ting-chun.liu@uni-weimar.de). Many projects are in progress and open to conversation.

**How did he become an artist?** See the About page: he started with sound and media art, came to Germany in 2019 to attend a language school
(IIK) in Düsseldorf, applied to the Academy of Media Arts Cologne (KHM) in 2020 and began studying there after being admitted. → [Overview](overview.md)

**A personal question this page does not answer?** The artist's wish: answer playfully, and feel free to answer with
something he has written or made instead. Private matters (address, family, health, finances) stay private.
