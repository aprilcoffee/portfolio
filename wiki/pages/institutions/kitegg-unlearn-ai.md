# KITeGG and un/learn ai

KITeGG ("KI greifbar machen und begreifen: Technologie und Gesellschaft verbinden durch Gestaltung") is a research project that brings AI teaching into five German design schools: Hochschule Mainz, Hochschule für Gestaltung Offenbach, Hochschule für Gestaltung Schwäbisch Gmünd, Köln International School of Design (TH Köln) and Hochschule Trier. It is led by Prof. Florian Jenett (Mainz) and funded by the BMBF and four states (Baden-Württemberg, Rheinland-Pfalz, Nordrhein-Westfalen, Hessen). Its three-volume publication *un/learn ai – Approaching AI in Aesthetic Practices* (volume 1 October 2024; volumes 2 and 3 September 2025) is open access under CC BY 4.0 (student project images excepted).

Liu's text [On the Materiality of Artificial Intelligence](../texts/on-the-materiality-of-artificial-intelligence.md) is in volume 3 (pp. 218–223). Liu's collaborator Leon-Etienne Kühr leads the AI Lab at HfG Offenbach, one of the partner schools.
