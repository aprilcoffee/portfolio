# Chaos Communication Congress

The annual congress of the Chaos Computer Club. Liu and Leon-Etienne Kühr spoke there three years in a row, each time in the Art & Beauty track; the recordings are free to watch (CC BY 4.0):

- **37C3 (2023), 30 December, 53 min:** *Self-cannibalizing AI – Artistic Strategies to Expose Generative Text-to-Image Models*. https://media.ccc.de/v/37c3-12125-self-cannibalizing_ai → [Self-cannibalizing AI](../works/self-cannibalizing-ai.md)
- **38C3 (2024), 28 December, 60 min, Saal ZIGZAG:** *arafed futures – An Artist Dialogue on Chip Storage and AI Accelerationism*. The word "arafed", with no dictionary meaning, spreads through AI captions and product texts, perhaps from the BLIP-2 captioning model; the talk sets this against semiconductor supply chains and NVIDIA A100 chips made by TSMC. https://media.ccc.de/v/38c3-arafed-futures-an-artist-dialogue-on-chip-storage-and-ai-accelerationism
- **39C3 (2025), 28–29 December, 38 min:** *51 Ways to Spell the Image Giraffe: The Hidden Politics of Token Languages in Generative AI*. Tokenisation encodes political and corporate choices before training: there are 51 ways to spell "giraffe" from tokens, and some images cannot be prompted because of sanitising in the token dictionary; experiments with genetic algorithms and edge cases. https://media.ccc.de/v/39c3-51-ways-to-spell-the-image-giraffe-the-hidden-politics-of-token-languages-in-generative-ai
