# Academy of Media Arts Cologne (KHM)

Kunsthochschule für Medien Köln. Liu completed a Postgraduate Diploma in Media and Fine Art there
(2020–2025, with distinction) and worked as a student assistant in Experimental Informatics
(2022–2024). The thesis *Heat as Generative Image Making* received the Weizenbaum-Studienpreis 2025.
Texts appeared on KHM's platform [ ] ground zero (2021, 2022): [Poetic Materialization – Regarding Midjourney](../texts/poetic-materialization-regarding-midjourney.md); the TEDA'24 poster with Kühr is on ground zero too (https://ground-zero.khm.de/artistic-research-on-recursive-dynamics-in-generative-image-models/). The thesis is [Heat as (Generative) Image Making](../texts/heat-as-generative-image-making.md). With the student group soft rotation, Liu was one of seven organisers of *soft rotation × moers festival* (6–9 June 2025, Moers) and performed on 8 June: https://en.khm.de/termine/news.5963.soft-rotation-x-moers-festival/ At the KHM Rundgang 2024, [ ] ground zero's show *Magic Wand* included a talk by Liu, "Chip shortage, acceleration and Taiwan's Dilemma": https://ground-zero.khm.de/portfolio/magic-wand/
