# FIfF e.V. and the Weizenbaum-Studienpreis

FIfF (Forum InformatikerInnen für Frieden und gesellschaftliche Verantwortung) awarded Liu the
Weizenbaum-Studienpreis 2025 for the postgraduate thesis *Heat as Generative Image Making*. The thesis text is summarised in [Heat as (Generative) Image Making](../texts/heat-as-generative-image-making.md). The essay
*Heat as (Generative) Image Making* appeared in FIfF-Kommunikation 1/26 (p. 64): https://www.fiff.de/fiff-kommunikation/2026/1/. About the prize (given in memory of Joseph Weizenbaum for work by young researchers on computer science and society): https://www.fiff.de/weizenbaum-preise/studienpreis/ Liu also took part in
*Aesthetic Approaches to Cyber Peace Work* at FIfFKon 2023, Berlin.
