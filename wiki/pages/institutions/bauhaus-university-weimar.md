# Bauhaus University Weimar

Liu has been Lecturer and Artistic Associate (Künstlerischer Mitarbeiter) at the Faculty of Art and
Design since 2025, after teaching as a guest lecturer (Lehrbeauftragter) at the Chair of Media
Environments in 2024, and gave a Bauhaus Master Lecture in 2025. The 2026 Stifterverband fellowship ([fellow page](https://www.stifterverband.org/digital-lehrfellows-thueringen/2026/liu))
(project "Lokale KI-Anwendung im Bewegtbild-Studio", until 2027) concerns digital teaching in Thuringia: a seminar for students of the Professur Crossmediales Bewegtbild working with local open-source tools (Ollama, LLaVA, CLIP), without cloud and without prior programming knowledge, including a visit to a data centre and ending in a public student exhibition.

Liu's staff page lists the role as Künstlerischer Mitarbeiter at the Professur Crossmediales Bewegtbild (Creative Technologist), Visuelle Kommunikation, Faculty of Art and Design: https://www.uni-weimar.de/de/kunst-und-gestaltung/struktur/lehrgebiete-personen/visuelle-kommunikation/ting-chun-liu/. The Weimar group *Cybernetic Subjects* showed Liu's [Latent Heat Generation](../works/latent-heat-generation.md) at the Ars Electronica Festival 2025.
