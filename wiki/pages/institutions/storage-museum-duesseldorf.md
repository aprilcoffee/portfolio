# Storage Museum Düsseldorf

A museum in Düsseldorf. It commissioned
[Interpolation of the Invisible Color](../works/interpolation-of-the-invisible-color.md) (2025, with Leon-Etienne Kühr) and hosted the workshop *Generating for the Archive* (28 November 2025) and the panel *Latenz* (20 January 2026), both organised with the [Filmwerkstatt Düsseldorf](filmwerkstatt-duesseldorf.md). The exhibition ran from 15 November 2025 to 31 January 2026 (Thursday–Sunday, 2–6 pm). Museum pages: https://storagemuseum.org/en/program/6-ausstellung · https://storagemuseum.org/en/database
