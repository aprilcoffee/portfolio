# Filmwerkstatt Düsseldorf

An art venue in Düsseldorf (Birkenstr. 47, Im Hof) that ran the *LATENT SPACE – LATENZRÄUME DER KUNST* programme. Liu and Leon-Etienne Kühr took part in four of its events around their [Storage Museum](storage-museum-duesseldorf.md) exhibition, and their text [Stereotype Encoding](../texts/stereotype-encoding.md) appeared in its publication *LATENT SPACE – LATENZ* (2025).

## Events with Liu and Kühr

- **Interpolation of Invisible Colors** (the organiser's spelling): opening 15 November 2025, 6 pm, running to 31 January 2026 at the Storage Museum, Thursday to Sunday, 2–6 pm. The page describes two ways of mapping the collection, physical storage that compresses by space and material, and generative models that compress the same works into high-dimensional vectors. Work page: [Interpolation of the Invisible Color](../works/interpolation-of-the-invisible-color.md). https://www.filmwerkstatt-duesseldorf.de/interpolation-of-invisible-colors/
- **Generating for the Archive:** workshop, Friday 28 November 2025, 11:00–18:00, Storage Museum; bilingual (English/German), no prior experience; worked with the museum's digitised collection. https://www.filmwerkstatt-duesseldorf.de/generating-for-the-archive/
- **Latent Space Symposium:** Saturday 29 November 2025, 13:00–22:00. Afternoon: Ludovica Schaerf, "Do Latent Spaces Still Exist?"; Liu and Kühr, "Stereotype Encoding"; Hedda Roman, "Künstler*innen als 'Rogue Agents'". https://www.filmwerkstatt-duesseldorf.de/latent-space-symposium/
- **Latenz:** panel with Q&A, Tuesday 20 January 2026, 6–7:30 pm, Storage Museum, with Dominik Bönisch-Alpári (cultural scientist, AI and archives) and Prof. Dr. Ludger Schwarte (philosophy, Kunstakademie Düsseldorf), on latency and latent spaces in art and AI in curating. https://www.filmwerkstatt-duesseldorf.de/latenz/

## Open questions
