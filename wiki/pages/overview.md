# Ting-Chun Liu (劉庭均) — overview

Ting-Chun Liu (劉庭均, born in Taipei, Taiwan) is a research-based media artist working with critical AI
(a critical AI artist) and an artistic researcher. His practice and research operate at the
intersections of critical artificial intelligence, audiovisual media and network practice. In the
artist's own words, the practice dissects "technological systems through feedback-driven
interventions", examining "how perception, aesthetics, and power dynamics propagate through
computational processes". Asked in 2026 what he does, he put it this way: he approaches AI art along media art's question of
**medium specificity** and asks how to go beyond simply prompting (see
[Medium specificity: the practice in his own words](topics/medium-specificity-and-practice.md)).

Liu came to Germany in 2019, began studying at the Academy of Media Arts Cologne in 2020 (as the pandemic began) and has
worked at Bauhaus University Weimar since the end of 2024; he works in Cologne and Weimar. He works mainly in English,
and also in Mandarin.

## At a glance

- **Practice:** installations, audio-visual performance, internet art, talks and artistic research,
  much of it about generative AI: image models, language models, datasets and the hardware they run on.
- **Workshops:** Liu hosts workshops about local AI usage, embedding practice and understanding biases in AI models, for anyone from people with no coding experience to artists, companies and literary scholars; what participants take away is mainly AI literacy. Requests go by email (see Contact); he is open to workshops, talks, collaborations and commissions.
- **Teaching:** Lecturer and Artistic Associate (Künstlerischer Mitarbeiter), Faculty of Art and Design,
  [Bauhaus University Weimar](institutions/bauhaus-university-weimar.md), since 2025.
- **Education:** Postgraduate Diploma in Media and Fine Art with distinction,
  [Academy of Media Arts Cologne (KHM)](institutions/khm-cologne.md), 2020–2025; B.F.A. in New Media Art,
  [Taipei National University of the Arts](institutions/tnua.md), 2014–2018.
- **Recognition:** Weizenbaum-Studienpreis 2025 ([FIfF e.V.](institutions/fiff.md)); 2026 fellowship of the
  Stifterverband for innovation in digital university teaching in Thuringia ([fellow page](https://www.stifterverband.org/digital-lehrfellows-thueringen/2026/liu)).
- **Frequent collaborator:** [Leon-Etienne Kühr](people/leon-etienne-kuehr.md), on installations, curatorial
  work and many talks, including three at the [Chaos Communication Congress](institutions/chaos-communication-congress.md).
