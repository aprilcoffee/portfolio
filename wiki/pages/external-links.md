# External links: pages by others about Ting-Chun Liu

Where Liu's work can be checked, watched or read outside his own website: talk recordings, texts, event and exhibition pages of organisers, institutional pages, and texts by others that cite him. Links taken over from the CV on the artist's website (marked CV) were not opened again; every other link was opened when this page was written. Pages on the artist's own domain are in [Site map and links](site-map.md). The website's Press & Media page (/press/) shows a selection of this list. Items are grouped by what a visitor wants to do.

## Watch and listen

- **39C3, 2025: *51 Ways to Spell the Image Giraffe: The Hidden Politics of Token Languages in Generative AI*** (with Leon-Etienne Kühr; 28–29 December 2025, 38 min, English with German translation; CC BY 4.0). Tokenisation as politics: there are 51 ways to spell "giraffe" from tokens, and some images cannot be prompted because of sanitising in the token dictionary. https://media.ccc.de/v/39c3-51-ways-to-spell-the-image-giraffe-the-hidden-politics-of-token-languages-in-generative-ai
- **38C3, 2024: *arafed futures – An Artist Dialogue on Chip Storage and AI Accelerationism*** (28 December 2024, 60 min, Saal ZIGZAG, English with German and Polish translation). "arafed" is a meaningless word that spread across captions and product texts, perhaps from the BLIP-2 captioning model, set beside NVIDIA A100 chips made by TSMC. https://media.ccc.de/v/38c3-arafed-futures-an-artist-dialogue-on-chip-storage-and-ai-accelerationism · speaker page: https://fahrplan.events.ccc.de/congress/2024/fahrplan/speaker/FDYRKA/
- **37C3, 2023: *Self-cannibalizing AI*** (30 December 2023, 53 min). Feedback, misuse and hacking on text-to-image models: dataset filtering, CLIP, recursive generation that turns images "into purple patterns or mundane concepts". https://media.ccc.de/v/37c3-12125-self-cannibalizing_ai → [Self-cannibalizing AI](works/self-cannibalizing-ai.md)
- **Screen Walk with Leon-Etienne Kühr and Ting-Chun Liu** (Screen Walks, Wednesday 26 March 2025): live-coded session on generative AI fed with its own output, "feedback as a strategy and cybernetics as a framework". Hosted by The Photographers' Gallery, London, and Fotomuseum Winterthur. https://thephotographersgallery.org.uk/whats-on/watch-screen-walk-leon-etienne-kuhr-and-ting-chun-liu · https://www.fotomuseum.ch/en/events-post/screen-walk-mit-leon-etienne-kuehr-und-ting-chun-liu/ · recording (embedded on the Photographers' Gallery page, not opened separately): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=13vt1I9I8_k
- **Performance videos** (Vimeo) are listed in [Site map and links](site-map.md#performance-videos). Steering Through the Inner Residue: https://vimeo.com/1171767792

## Cited or mentioned by others

- **Violaine Boutet de Monvel, "Model Collapse: On Recursion, Noise, and Uncharted Machine Visions"** (arXiv:2607.09705, a media-archaeology essay comparing analog video feedback with recursive AI training; author at LIRA/IRCAV; posted June 2026). In the conclusion she writes that Liu and Kühr "have created feedback loops within BLIP-2, an image-captioning model that generates descriptions from visual data", and "have also tested methods for collapsing Stable Diffusion without extended retraining or even textual prompting". The reference list cites *Arafed Futures* (ground zero) and the TEDA poster. Reading: the pair are placed as artists working on the same ground as the paper, recursion and noise. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09705 · review page: https://pith.science/paper/2607.09705
- **Ars Electronica Festival 2025, exhibition *Cybernetic Subjects*** (Bauhaus-Universität Weimar, University of Arts Linz, curated by Ursula Damm, Kerstin Ergenzinger and Mindaugas Gapševičius): Liu's *Latent Heat Generation* is one of the works, described as investigating "the hidden material infrastructures of AI systems through thermal imaging feedback loops". https://ars.electronica.art/panic/en/view/cybernetic-subjects-21338ddb450c817ab718ce48a0a628f5/ → [Latent Heat Generation](works/latent-heat-generation.md)
