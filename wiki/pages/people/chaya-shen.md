# Chaya Shen

Artist, born in Taiwan, who lives and works in Cologne. She is Ting-Chun Liu's partner (his significant
other, as Liu states). Her website: https://chayashen.info/

## Practice

In her own words, her work focuses on "female body, the materiality, and the surrounding
interrelations". She researches by appropriating "constructed apparatus and measurements around
body-technology relations and beyond". Her projects span installation, performance, sound and
writing. Recurring threads: medical imaging and the body, care and tenderness between people and
machines, and generative AI translating images of the body.

## Selected works

- ***I make the babies it gave me*** (2025): a twelve-part metal sheet work made from the artist's own
  ultrasound images; the body image was translated three times by generative AI models. First
  window of the KHM seminar *Urban Stage* (Prof. Mischa Kuball) at Kunsthaus Lempertz, Cologne, from
  27 May 2025. https://en.khm.de/termine/news.5965.lempertz-1845-art-chaya-shen/
- ***ChatGPT give birth my babies*** (KHM Rundgang 2024, [ ] ground zero): asks what we see in an
  ultrasound image; in digital medicine the patient is "anonymized, analyzed and diagnosed", and the
  gap between the image of a body and its lived meaning shows in clinical practice.
  https://ground-zero.khm.de/portfolio/magic-wand/
- **Menstrual cups** (installation, July 2023): a pile of fragile, hand-made, broken wax cups,
  smaller than those on the market, left in the corner of a room. Described in her essay below.

## Writing

- ***Die Behandlung, Tenderness of the In-between*** (essay, published as Chia-Ying Shen by the
  Conflict, Justice, Decolonization project, 29 March 2025). Women's bodies and medical production in
  Taiwan's colonial history: missionary medicine, Japanese medical modernity and the later health
  system. Starting from buying a menstrual cup in Germany, she reads globalised medical products,
  with Yuk Hui, as "unilateral globalization", and borrows Gadamer's *Behandlung* (both "handling"
  and "hand") to ask for a treatment beyond modern technique. Keywords: woman's body, machine, care.
  https://cjdproject.web.nycu.edu.tw/2025/03/29/die-behandlung-tenderness-of-the-in-between/
- **Substack** (her "lighthearted little murmuring garden"): https://goodwingbaby.substack.com (not
  read: the page blocks automatic access).
- Lectures: *Alignment After Synthetic Sentience* (Politics of the Machines, Perth, 2025); *What is
  care? Tenderness of the machine* (Code & Context, TH Köln, 2025); *Human touch/ Human's touch*
  ([ ] ground zero, KHM Rundgang, 6 July 2024). Workshop *Vom Körper zu Kunst* (Bonn, 2025).

## Shared contexts with Liu

- Both are connected to the [Academy of Media Arts Cologne (KHM)](../institutions/khm-cologne.md): Liu
  studied there (2020–2025); Shen showed work in KHM student seminars and shows. Both took part in
  [ ] ground zero's *Magic Wand* show at the KHM Rundgang 2024, where Liu spoke on "Chip shortage,
  acceleration and Taiwan's Dilemma" and Shen on "Human touch/ Human's touch".
- She co-organises the concert series *soft rotation* with friends (https://softrotation.substack.com);
  Liu was one of the organisers of *soft rotation × moers festival* (2025).
