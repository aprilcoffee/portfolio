# Questionnaire 2026-10 (b): the artist's answers

Answers given by Ting-Chun Liu on 25 October 2026 to questions visitors ask the website chat
(numbers as in the questionnaire; skipped questions left out; a private detail about residence status left out on purpose).

1. Favourite ice cream: sea salt (海鹽).
3. Favourite city: Cologne.
4. Reading at the moment: Vilém Flusser.
5. Outside work: plays Hearthstone.
7. A quote he likes, from Sol LeWitt (he remembered it as "a flower painted is not a flower, a line drawn is a line"); the attested wording is "Obviously a drawing of a person is not a real person, but a drawing of a line is a real line."
8. Why AI: he started by playing with natural language processing (NLP); in 2022 he used the earliest DALL·E and found the idea of combining text and images interesting.
10. Hardest to explain: 51 Ways to Spell the Image Giraffe, because there are so many technical questions to describe; in fact all the works need a lot of prior knowledge to be understood.
11. How a work starts: from the aesthetics of a technical framework.
12. Tools: all of them (p5.js, Processing, Python, TouchDesigner and others).
13. With Leon-Etienne Kühr: they discuss with each other and write code together.
14. Many projects are in progress; open to talking about them.
15. Uses generative AI himself when writing code.
16. Will AI replace artists? No. Artists adapt to new technologies and aesthetics; whatever happens, they have to stay experimental and must not be bound by the technology.
17. Methods besides feedback: research context, historical stories, experiments and tests, hacking, overtuning.
18. On semiconductors and geopolitics: it is a contradictory concept, not simply about the environment or labour; geopolitics is in it too. Artists should not deal with only a single issue, but discuss this kind of many-sided contradiction; that is something only art can do.
19. Local or cloud models: uses both; local small models mainly in teaching, because students have no budget and should still be able to use AI.
20. Workshops: on participants' computers or in the cloud; mainly in English and Chinese; length negotiable.
21. Loans, commissions, collections: yes, by email.
22. Students: can write with any question.
23. Citing works and using images: yes, get in touch.
24. Interviews and podcasts: yes, get in touch.
25. Personal questions the knowledge base does not cover: answer playfully, and the chat may answer with anything he has written.
26. Topics: everything can be discussed (private data still stays private).
27. Personal path: see the About page; he started with sound and media art, came to Germany in 2019 to attend the language school IIK in Düsseldorf, applied to the Academy of Media Arts Cologne (KHM) in 2020 and began studying there once admitted.
