Workshop

Condition for Emotions

Run small AI models on your own laptop and ask them absurd questions, to see how a machine turns meaning into distances.

Language models work with embeddings: every word, sentence or image becomes a long list of numbers, a position in a space. Distances in that space tell a computer how close two things are. Correlation is not causation, but even arbitrary links can reveal unexpected associations.

Asking

Participants install small AI models, run them on their own laptops and ask them things like “How ice cream is the word red compared to blue?” or “How much of the smell after the first spring rain is in this image?” The answers may be absurd. They still show how a machine processes meaning, and how we read its logic through our own experience.

Measuring

With a small web tool built for the workshop, participants
· measure the distance between sentences
· place words on a line between two opposites
· compare how many tokens the same phrase costs in different languages
· follow a model as it continues a sentence word by word
· swap one name in a sentence and watch the continuation change
· ask a model questions about an image
