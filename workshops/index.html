Talks & Workshops

Workshops I offer: using AI locally on your own computer, working hands-on with embeddings, and seeing the biases inside AI models. They work for anyone, from people who have never written code to artists, companies and scholars. Talk recordings below.

Conditions for EmotionsSmall language models on your own laptop, absurd questions, confident answers: a workshop on the gap between what a machine computes and the meaning we read into it.
Paranoia De-Promptingwith Leon-Etienne KührGet to know CLIP, the model that decides whether a picture “matches” a text, and watch what it imagines when it hears “happy” or “nurse”.
The Given ImageA game of telephone for images: each group sees only the picture handed to it, describes it, and lets AI redraw it from the words. Round after round, the image drifts.
