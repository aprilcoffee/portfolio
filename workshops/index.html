Workshops

Conditions for EmotionsSmall language models on your own laptop, absurd questions, confident answers: a workshop on the gap between what a machine computes and the meaning we read into it.
51 Ways to Spell the Image Giraffewith Leon-Etienne KührWhat does an image generator actually read? Look up words in its token dictionary, type prompts as numbers, and rebuild an image from tokens alone.
Stereotype Encodingwith Leon-Etienne KührGet to know CLIP, the model that decides whether a picture “matches” a text, and watch what it imagines when it hears “happy” or “nurse”.
The Given ImageA game of telephone for images: each group sees only the picture handed to it, describes it, and lets AI redraw it from the words. Round after round, the image drifts.

51 Ways to Spell the Image Giraffe: The Hidden Politics of Token Languages in Generative AI

39C3, media.ccc.de · 2025

Talk with Leon-Etienne Kühr, in English with German translation. Before an image model reads a prompt, a tokenizer cuts the text into pieces from a fixed dictionary: “giraffe” can be spelled from tokens in 51 ways, and some images cannot be prompted at all because words were cleaned out of that dictionary. The talk reads tokenization as politics, decided before any training begins.

Screen Walk with Leon-Etienne Kühr and Ting-Chun Liu

The Photographers’ Gallery, London, with Fotomuseum Winterthur · 2025

Online Screen Walk for The Photographers’ Gallery and Fotomuseum Winterthur, 26 March 2025. Live-coding on screen, Kühr and Liu feed generative models their own output and watch where the images drift: feedback as an artistic strategy, cybernetics as the frame. The recording is on the gallery’s page.

arafed futures – An Artist Dialogue on Chip Storage and AI Accelerationism

38C3, media.ccc.de · 2024

Artist dialogue with Leon-Etienne Kühr. It starts from the chip shortage of the pandemic years and the load generative AI puts on hardware, then follows “arafed”, a meaningless word made by captioning models that has spread into image captions and product texts. These oddities of the embedding space are tied to the geopolitics of semiconductor supply chains, with NVIDIA A100 chips made by TSMC as the case.

Self-cannibalizing AI

37C3, media.ccc.de · 2023

Talk with Leon-Etienne Kühr. What happens when machines learn from each other and feed on their own output? Can an image model pick the happiest person in a photo, or guess ethnicity from a random image? With feedback, misuse and hacking, the talk takes text-to-image pipelines apart: dataset filtering, labelling with CLIP, and recursive generation that turns images into purple patterns or mundane concepts.
