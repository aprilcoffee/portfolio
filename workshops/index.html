Workshops & Talks

Workshops on local AI usage, embedding practice and understanding biases in AI models, and recordings of talks.

Condition for EmotionsRun small AI models on your own laptop and ask them absurd questions, to see how a machine turns meaning into distances.
Paranoia De-Promptingwith Leon-Etienne KührPlay with CLIP, the model that decides which picture matches which words, and watch its idea of “happy” or “nurse” slowly appear.
The Given ImageA game of telephone (Stille Post) for images: each group describes a picture, a generator redraws it, and every step loses something and adds something.
