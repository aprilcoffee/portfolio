Workshop

The Given Image

A game of telephone for images: each group sees only the picture handed to it, describes it, and lets AI redraw it from the words. Round after round, the image drifts.

How to play

Participants work in groups, and each group starts with one image. They write a prompt that describes it, and an image generator makes a new picture from that text alone, without seeing the image. The new picture goes to the next group, who see neither the prompt nor the original, only the picture in front of them, and the round repeats. Next to each generated picture, a CLIP similarity score shows how well the machine thinks picture and words match.

Where it ends up

Every round loses some details and adds things that were never there. In a run in April 2026 (8 groups, 5 rounds), a creamy meatball stew turned into a yellow, then an orange soup; seven meatballs became one; potatoes, a fork and small bowls of cream appeared. In another chain, the outdoor seating of an ice-cream café ended as a courtyard of turquoise chairs between brick and glass buildings. Nobody did anything wrong: each step is just someone describing what they see.

Open source

The game is also part of my teaching at Bauhaus-Universität Weimar. The web app is open source: [github.com/aprilcoffee/ai-stille-post](https://github.com/aprilcoffee/ai-stille-post/tree/main/stille_post).
