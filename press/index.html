Talks and recordings

2025-1251 Ways to Spell the Image Giraffe: The Hidden Politics of Token Languages in Generative AI39C3, media.ccc.deTalk with Leon-Etienne Kühr, 38 min, English with German translation, CC BY 4.0. 51 spellings of “giraffe” from tokens, and what the token dictionary decides before any training.
2025-03Screen Walk with Leon-Etienne Kühr and Ting-Chun LiuThe Photographers’ Gallery, London, with Fotomuseum WinterthurLive-coded session on generative AI fed with its own output: feedback as a strategy and cybernetics as a framework. Recording on the gallery’s page.
2024-12arafed futures – An Artist Dialogue on Chip Storage and AI Accelerationism38C3, media.ccc.deArtist dialogue with Leon-Etienne Kühr, 60 min. “arafed” is a meaningless word that spread across captions and product texts, set beside NVIDIA A100 chips made by TSMC.
2023-12Self-cannibalizing AI37C3, media.ccc.deTalk with Leon-Etienne Kühr, 53 min. Feedback, misuse and hacking on text-to-image models: dataset filtering, CLIP, recursive generation.

Texts about the work

2026-06Model Collapse: On Recursion, Noise, and Uncharted Machine VisionsViolaine Boutet de Monvel, arXiv:2607.09705A media-archaeology essay comparing analog video feedback with recursive AI training. It cites the 38C3 talk and the TEDA poster, and describes the feedback loops of Liu and Kühr in BLIP-2 and Stable Diffusion.
2025-01Self-cannibalizing AI: Artistic Strategies to expose generative text-to-image modelsImage Exhaust, Unthinking Photography (The Photographers’ Gallery)Entry on the 37C3 talk, credited to Ting-Chun Liu and Leon-Etienne Kühr: artistic methods for examining image-generation models, and how model collapse could happen through the repeated use of generated material.
2024-11The Autophagic Mode of Production: Hacking the Metabolism of AILuca Cacini, A Peer-Reviewed Journal About (APRJA) 13(1), pp. 78–89Journal article that cites the 37C3 talk and discusses Liu and Kühr’s feedback loop as an artistic strategy for investigating the latent space of machine learning.
